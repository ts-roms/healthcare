# Data Privacy Act (RA 10173): what the platform does, and what stays with the organization

The Data Privacy Act of 2012, its implementing rules and the National Privacy Commission's (NPC) circulars govern how a
clinic or hospital processes personal and sensitive personal information — health information is sensitive personal
information. This page maps each obligation the platform can support to what is built, and says plainly what remains
the organization's to do. It encodes **no rule from memory** (CLAUDE.md §36): periods, thresholds, wording and
decisions are the organization's, entered as its own settings and validated by its Data Protection Officer (DPO). It
is not a claim of compliance; the organization assesses that against the current official text. Record each
validation under **Administration → Compliance** (`data_privacy` area).

| Obligation (as the organization reads it in the Act and the NPC's issuances)                            | What the platform does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | What the organization does and validates                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Lawful processing and consent** (criteria for processing; consent as one basis)                       | Append-only consent history per patient (`patient_consent`: data processing, treatment, telemedicine, HMO and PhilHealth sharing, portal access, research; granted, refused, withdrawn; paper, electronic or verbal; an optional scanned form); the organization's own consent wording, versioned, read before a consent given online (`consent_text`); withdrawal in MyHealth; outreach only under an explicit per-channel opt-in; notices respect preferences; the research consent is never assumed ([patient](../domains/patient.md), [crm](../domains/crm.md))                                                                                                  | Which basis applies to each processing (treatment, legal obligation, consent …); the consent and privacy-notice wording; whether electronic consent and online withdrawal meet its legal needs; who may consent for a minor or a dependent |
| **Right to be informed** (privacy notice; the DPO's contact)                                            | Consent wording shown before each online consent; the organization's own notice shown before a records request; the DPO's name and contact, when set (`privacy_setting`, migration `0114`), shown to patients in MyHealth under **Privacy and consents**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | The privacy notice itself (purpose, recipients, retention, rights) and where it is published; the DPO's appointment                                                                                                                        |
| **Right of access** (a copy of the personal data)                                                       | Records requests in MyHealth (`records_request`, kind `access`): what, period, purpose; the records office shares documents or a compiled **copy of the record** (PDF of the structured record) or declines with a reason the patient reads; the organization's own response time, identity check and notice ([records requests](../domains/records-requests.md)); released results, prescriptions, care plans, certificates, referrals, immunizations and the reported history are read directly in MyHealth                                                                                                                                                        | Response times, fees, identity checks and what may be withheld; whether a copy must be certified                                                                                                                                           |
| **Right to rectification, erasure or blocking, and to object**                                          | Requests of kind `rectification`, `erasure` and `objection` in the same workflow (migration `0114`): the patient says what and why; the records office answers with what was done, or declines with a reason. **The platform changes or deletes nothing on a request**: corrections go through the ordinary screens (patient details, "entered in error", amendments), objections through communication preferences or consent withdrawal, erasure through the organization's archival procedure — then the answer is recorded                                                                                                                                       | Whether and how a clinical record may be erased or blocked (medical-record retention law may forbid it), the procedure for corrections to a signed record, and the response time                                                           |
| **Data portability**                                                                                    | A copy of the record as PDF; FHIR R4 read access for systems the organization authorizes (`interop.fhir.read`, [fhir](../interoperability/fhir.md)); CSV exports of operational tables are audited                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Whether and in what format portability is offered to patients (a structured export to the patient is not built)                                                                                                                            |
| **Security measures — organizational**                                                                  | Roles and permissions scoped by organization, facility and department; audit trail of sensitive reads and every change (append-only, partitioned, archived with checksums); a register of who validated each compliance area; this page                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | The DPO, the privacy management programme, policies, training, registration with the NPC where required, privacy impact assessments, data-sharing and outsourcing agreements (hosting, SMS, email, video)                                  |
| **Security measures — physical and technical**                                                          | Password rules and breached-password screening; staff two-step verification (optionally required) and MyHealth two-step verification (optionally required), passkeys; sessions with refresh and revocation; **idle sign-out of the staff app** (`STAFF_IDLE_MINUTES`, default 20 minutes, a warning first); rate limits; row-level security by organization in the database; a restricted database role that cannot update or delete append-only history; documents in private object storage with server-side encryption, signed short links, malware scanning, checksums and an integrity review; logs, events and push messages carry ids, never clinical content | Encryption at rest and backups at the hosting provider, network and endpoint security, physical access, the handling of printed records and shared computers (the idle period is theirs to set), penetration testing                       |
| **Personal data breach management** (assessment, notification of the NPC and of data subjects, records) | The **breach register** (`privacy_incident`, migration `0114`; staff **Administration → Data privacy**, `privacy.incident.manage`): each incident with its discovery time, the data involved, an estimate of those affected, an append-only trail of what was done; the assessment (notifiable or not, with the reason); the NPC notification and the notification of the affected people as recorded; closure only after assessment; the organization's own notification window counted from discovery, flagged when passed                                                                                                                                         | Whether an incident is notifiable, the notifications themselves (the platform sends none), the content of a notification, the window (the NPC's circular is the reference) and the incident-response procedure                             |
| **Retention and disposal**                                                                              | Clinical records are never casually deleted (statuses, amendments, entered in error, append-only history); document retention periods per category with a review of what is past them (nothing deleted); audit months archived to object storage and removable only past a configured period with a reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Retention periods per record type, disposal or anonymization procedures, legal holds                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Accountability for transfers and processors**                                                         | Every outbound exchange is sealed, logged by digest and reviewable; adapters for PhilHealth, DOH and reference laboratories are unconfigured until the organization connects them; outreach, SMS, email, push and video go through providers the organization chooses                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Data-sharing agreements and outsourcing contracts with each provider; the NPC's requirements for cross-border transfer where a provider is abroad                                                                                          |
