# 12. MyHealth — your patient portal

**What this is for.** MyHealth is your clinic's website for patients. On your phone or computer you can see your upcoming and past visits, book, move
or cancel a visit, answer the questions before an online consultation and join the video call, read your laboratory results in plain language, see
your active prescriptions and care plan, check your bills, and read messages from the clinic. If your clinic shares dental records, you can also see
your dental treatment plans and tooth chart.

**Who uses it.** Patients of the clinic who have been given an activation code at the clinic. You can only see your own record. You cannot change your
record in MyHealth — clinic staff make corrections so your record stays accurate.

> **In an emergency, do not use MyHealth. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.** Messages in MyHealth are one-way, and online booking is not
> for urgent care.

## How to set up your account (first time)

You need an **activation code** from the clinic. Ask at the front desk. Staff check who you are in person, record your consent to use MyHealth, and
give you a code of 10 letters and numbers (for example `ABCDE-23456`). The code works for **3 days** and only once.

1. Open MyHealth and choose **Set up your account** (under **First time here?** on the sign-in page).
2. Under **1. Confirm it's you**, enter:
   - **Patient number** — printed on your clinic card or receipt.
   - **Date of birth**.
   - **Activation code** — dashes and spaces do not matter.
3. Under **2. Choose how you'll sign in**, enter your **Email**, a **New password** and **Type the password again**. The password must have at least
   12 characters and not be too repetitive. A short phrase is easy to remember.
4. Choose **Set up and sign in**.

You arrive on the Home page with the message "Your account is ready. Next time, sign in with …" and your email.

If any detail does not match, you see "Activation details are incorrect, or the code has expired. Ask the clinic for a new code." For your privacy, the
page does not say which detail was wrong. Clinic staff can see why the last attempt failed, so ask them. After too many wrong attempts the code stops
working and you need a new one.

## How to sign in and sign out

1. Open MyHealth. On **Sign in**, enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Choose **Sign in**.

To sign out, choose **Sign out** at the top of the page. You see "You have signed out."

If you have been away for a while, or the clinic ended your access, you may be sent back to the sign-in page with "You were signed out. Sign in again to
continue."

**Forgot your password?** There is no password reset in MyHealth yet. Ask the clinic for a new activation code, then set up your account again.

## Finding your way around

The menu has:

| Menu item    | What you find there                                                              |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**     | A greeting, quick buttons, your next visits and your most recent results         |
| **Visits**   | Upcoming visits and visits from the past year; book, change or cancel            |
| **Results**  | Your laboratory results and printable reports                                    |
| **Dental**   | Only shown when your clinic shares dental records and there is something to show |
| **Messages** | Notices and messages from the clinic. A number shows how many are new            |
| **Profile**  | Your name, patient number, date of birth, sex, clinic and sign-in email          |

On **Home**, the quick buttons are **Book appointment**, **Care plan**, **Lab results**, **Prescriptions** and **Bills**. Your care plan, medicines
and bills are opened from these buttons. **See all** next to **Upcoming** or **Recent results** opens the full list.

Times of visits are shown in the clinic's local time (Philippine time for most clinics).

If you open a part of MyHealth that is not built yet, you see "Coming soon".

## How to see your visits

1. Choose **Visits**.
2. **Upcoming** lists visits you or the clinic booked. **Past year** lists earlier visits.

Each visit shows the date and time, the kind of visit, the doctor, and the clinic (or **Online** for an online consultation). The status is one of
**Booked**, **Confirmed**, **Checked in**, **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **Missed**.

## How to book a visit online

You can only book the kinds of visit your clinic has opened for online booking. If the clinic has not opened any, you see "Online booking is not
available" — please call the clinic.

1. Choose **Book appointment** on Home, or **Book a visit** on **Visits**.
2. **What kind of visit?** — choose one. Each shows whether it is **At the clinic** or **Online, by video**, and about how long it takes.
3. **Where?** — choose the clinic branch (only asked if there is more than one).
4. **Which doctor?** — choose **Any available doctor** or a doctor by name.
5. **When?** — choose a day, then an open time under **Morning** or **Afternoon**. If you chose "any doctor", each time shows the doctor's name. If a
   day is full you see "No open times on this day. Try another day."
6. **Confirm** — check the date, time, visit and place. You may add a **Reason for the visit (optional)**. Only the clinic sees this. Do not use it
   for emergencies.
7. Choose **Book this time**.

You see "Your visit is booked." The clinic sends you a confirmation and, later, a reminder.

For an online visit, note: not every concern can be handled online. Your doctor may ask you to come to the clinic.

## How to change or cancel a visit

Below an upcoming visit you can change, choose **Change or cancel** (or **Cancel** if the visit can only be cancelled).

**To move it to another time** (same doctor):

1. Under **Choose a new time**, choose a day and an open time.
2. Choose the button **Move to …** with the new time.

You see "Your visit was moved." To see a different doctor, cancel and book again.

**To cancel it:**

1. Under **Cancel this visit**, choose **Cancel visit**.
2. Optionally say **Why are you cancelling? (optional)**.
3. Choose **Yes, cancel the visit** (or **Keep the visit** to go back).

You see "Your visit was cancelled." The time is released for other patients.

Online changes close 2 hours before the visit. After that you see "Please call the clinic — This visit can no longer be changed online."

## How to attend an online consultation

Open the online visit from **Home** or **Visits** (it says "Answer the questions and join from here"). The page takes you step by step.

**1. Answer the questions (Before your consultation).** Your doctor reads your answers before the call.

1. Fill in **Why are you consulting?** (required), **What symptoms do you have?**, **For how many days?**, **Medicines you are taking now** (include
   vitamins and herbal products) and **Any new allergies since your last visit?**
2. Under **Do you have any of these right now?**, tick any that apply (for example chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, fainting, signs of
   stroke, a seizure, sudden severe pain, pregnancy with bleeding or pain, thoughts of harming yourself).
   If you tick any of them you see: "This may be an emergency. Do not wait for an online consultation." **Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room
   now.** You can still send your answers so your doctor knows.
3. Enter **Where will you be during the call? (city or municipality)** and **A number we can call if the video fails**. Both are required.
4. Tick the statement that an online consultation cannot include a physical examination, that your doctor may ask you to come in, and that you should
   use a private place. The call is not recorded.
5. Choose **Send my answers**.

**2. Wait for the waiting room to open.** You see "Your answers are with your doctor". The waiting room opens 30 minutes before your visit time. Keep the
page open or come back then.

**3. Enter the waiting room.** When you see **Ready when you are**, go to a quiet, private place with good signal and choose **Enter the waiting
room**. The clinic now knows you are there. The page updates by itself while you wait.

**4. Join the call.** When the doctor starts, you see "… is ready". Choose **Join the video call** and allow your browser to use your camera and
microphone. If video is not available, you see "Your doctor will call you" — keep your phone nearby; the doctor calls the number you gave.

**5. After the call.** You see "Your consultation has ended" with **Your doctor's instructions**, if the doctor wrote any. If the doctor decides you
need to be examined in person, you see "Your doctor recommends seeing you in person". Please follow the instructions and visit the clinic.

If the visit was cancelled, missed or has passed, you see "This consultation is no longer available". Contact the clinic to book another.

## How to read your laboratory results

1. Choose **Results** (or **Lab results** on Home).
2. Each test shows its latest value, the date, and where the value sits against the usual range, in words and with an icon:
   - **Within the usual range**
   - **Higher than the usual range** / **Lower than the usual range**
   - **Outside what is usually expected**
   - **Much higher (or lower) than the usual range — your care team has been told**
   - **No usual range to compare with**
3. Under each result you may see **Usual range: …**, how many results you have over time, "updated by the laboratory" (the laboratory corrected it) and
   "tested at …" (another laboratory performed the test).
4. Choose a test to open it. You see the latest value, a chart **Over time** (the shaded band is the usual range), and the **History** with each
   value's usual range at the time.
5. Under **Printable reports**, choose **Report …** to open the laboratory report as a PDF.

MyHealth explains where a value sits; it does not tell you what the result means for your health. **Talk to your doctor** about your results.

**When do results appear?** Only after the laboratory has checked and released them, and only for tests the clinic allows patients to see online. A
result far outside the usual range is shown only after your care team has seen it. While the laboratory corrects a result, it is hidden until the
corrected result is released. Some tests are explained by your doctor in person first.

When new results are ready you get a text message (or an email) saying results are available. It never names the test or the value — sign in to see
them.

## How to see your medicines

1. On Home, choose **Prescriptions**.
2. **Your medicines** lists your **active** prescriptions: the medicine, strength and brand, how to take it (for example "1 tablet twice a day for 7
   days"), your doctor's instructions, the quantity and refills, the doctor, the date and the prescription number.

Always follow your doctor's instructions. Ask your pharmacist or doctor if you are unsure. Prescriptions that ended or were cancelled are not shown.

## How to see your care plan

1. On Home, choose **Care plan**.
2. **Your care plan** shows each active plan with its **Goals**, **What you can do** and **Coming up with your care team**, with dates.

If you have no active care plan you see "No active care plan".

## How to see and pay your bills

1. On Home, choose **Bills**.
2. At the top, **Still to pay** is the total you owe across your invoices.
3. If you have paid a deposit or have credit, **Deposit and credit at …** shows your balance at that branch. Choose **Show history** to see each
   entry. The cashier applies this to your bills at that branch, or refunds it when you ask.
4. Each invoice shows its number, date, amount and status: **To pay**, **Partly paid**, **Paid**, or **Cancelled — replaced or corrected by the
   clinic**.
5. Choose **Show details** to see the items, discounts, what your HMO or PhilHealth covers (and whether that is **awaiting approval**, **claim
   submitted**, **paid by the payer** or **not covered — ask the clinic**), **Your share**, payments, deposit used, and any credit or debit notes.
6. Choose **Download PDF** for the invoice, or the links for any **Credit note** or **Debit note**.

**Paying.** Pay at the clinic's cashier. Paying online is only offered when your clinic has connected a payment provider; otherwise the page says
"paying online is not available yet". When it is available, an unpaid invoice shows **Pay ₱… online**, which takes you to the provider's payment page.
Your bill updates once the provider confirms the payment.

Questions about a bill? Ask the clinic's cashier and bring your invoice number.

## How to read messages

1. Choose **Messages**. The number next to it shows new messages.
2. Messages are listed newest first. New ones are marked **New** and count as read once you have seen them.
3. Some messages have a link such as **See your results**, **See your visits** or **Book a visit**.

You may receive booking confirmations, reminders, results-ready notices, "we missed you" notices after a missed visit, care plan follow-up reminders
and messages from clinic staff.

You cannot reply in MyHealth yet. Call the clinic for questions, and 911 in an emergency. To stop reminders by SMS or email, ask the clinic to update
your communication preferences.

## How to see your dental record

**Dental** appears in the menu only if your clinic shares dental records in MyHealth and there is something to show.

1. Choose **Dental**.
2. **Treatment plans** — each plan your dentist proposed, with each treatment, the tooth and sides, and its status (for example **Waiting for your
   decision**, **You agreed — not done yet**, **Done**, **You declined**, **No longer planned**).
3. **Treatments done** — the date, treatment, tooth, dentist and clinic.
4. **Your teeth** — a chart as your dentist last charted them, with a key (**No problems noted**, **Treated**, **Needs treatment**, **Being watched**,
   **Missing**, **Not charted**) and a list of teeth with something noted. You face the chart: your right side is on the left.
5. **X-rays and photos** — only images your dentist chose to share with you. Choose **Open** to view one. The clinic can see that you opened it.

Your dentist's notes are not shown here. If your clinic shows fee estimates, a plan shows the **Estimated fee** of each treatment still ahead and
the **Estimated cost of the treatment still ahead**, with the date of the prices. It is an estimate, not a bill: discounts and HMO or PhilHealth
coverage are not included, and a treatment without a listed price says "ask the clinic". Otherwise, ask the clinic about costs.

**Deciding on a treatment plan online.** Some clinics let you decide in MyHealth. If yours does, a plan waiting for your decision shows **Your
decision**:

1. Tick the treatments you want to go ahead with. Anything you leave unticked is declined.
2. Read the summary (for example "You accept … and decline …"). If your clinic shows estimates, you also see the estimated fee of what you
   accept.
3. Tick the clinic's statement to confirm it.
4. Choose **Send my decision**.

If the clinic's prices changed while you were deciding, you see "The clinic's prices changed since you opened this plan" and the new estimate;
decide again. If your clinic does not allow online decisions, you see "To decide, talk to your dentist or the clinic — decisions are recorded at the clinic."

## How to check your profile

Choose **Profile** to see your **Name**, **Patient number**, **Date of birth**, **Sex**, **Clinic** and **Sign-in email**. If something is wrong,
ask the clinic to correct your record.

## Rules the system enforces

- You can only set up an account with a valid, unused activation code, your patient number and your date of birth. The code expires after 3 days.
- Your clinic must have your consent on file. If it is withdrawn, you are signed out and cannot sign in.
- After 5 wrong passwords in a row, sign-in is locked for about 15 minutes.
- You can book only kinds of visit the clinic opened for online booking, only in open times of the doctor's schedule.
- You must book at least 2 hours ahead, and not more than 60 days ahead.
- You can have at most 3 open online bookings at a time.
- Online changes and cancellations close 2 hours before the visit. A time can only be moved with the same doctor.
- For an online consultation, you must answer the questions before you can enter the waiting room, and the video opens only when the doctor starts.
- Only released results the clinic allows patients to see are shown; results far outside the usual range appear only after your care team has seen
  them.
- Messages are one-way.
- You cannot edit your record, prescriptions or bills in MyHealth.

## For clinic staff

- Staff invite patients and disable access from the patient record, in the portal access panel (**Invite to portal**, **Issue new code**, **Disable
  access**). This needs the `patient.portal.manage` permission (by default: receptionist, records officer, organization administrator). The patient's
  `portal_access` consent must be recorded first. See [Patients](02-patients.md).
- A patient with an **active** account who forgot the password: **Disable access** (with a reason), then **Issue new code**.
- Dental sharing and online plan decisions are organization settings under `/dental/settings`. See [Dental](08-dental.md).
- Which tests patients may see is set per test in the laboratory catalog ("Released results may be shown to the patient"). See
  [Laboratory](06-laboratory.md).
